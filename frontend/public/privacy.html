Privacy Policy

Stratum Industries · Effective date: July 1, 2026 · Contact: info@stratum.industries

1. What MEMAR is

MEMAR is self-hosted fleet monitoring software. You deploy it on your own infrastructure. Your data never leaves your server unless you explicitly configure an integration that sends data outward.

2. Data we collect and where it lives

All of the following data is stored exclusively on your server in the SQLite database at the path you configure (MEMAR_DATA_DIR):

  • Account data: username, hashed password, role, session tokens.
  • Fleet data: printer/machine names, configurations, telemetry samples (temperatures, job progress, status), job logs, CNC metrics, anomaly records.
  • Audit log: admin actions with timestamps.
  • License record: your license key, tier, expiry, Stripe customer ID (opaque identifier — no payment card data).
  • Webhook configs: outbound webhook URLs you configure.

We do not have access to any of this data. We do not run a cloud backend. We do not collect telemetry or analytics.

3. Third-party services

MEMAR integrates with the following third parties only when you configure them or purchase a license:

  • Stripe — payment processing for license purchases. Stripe receives your email address and payment information directly. See stripe.com/privacy. We receive a Stripe Customer ID and subscription status; we never see your card number.
  • Cloudflare Workers — our license fulfillment worker receives the Stripe checkout event and emails you your license key. The worker does not store your data beyond the immediate transaction.
  • Resend (email delivery) — used by our Cloudflare Worker to send license keys and billing receipts to your email address. See resend.com/privacy.
  • ntfy / Slack / Telegram / webhooks — only if you configure outbound notifications. Data sent is alert content you choose to route (machine name, status). We have no visibility into your notification config or the data sent.
  • Your own SMTP server — only if you configure MEMAR_SMTP_*. We have no access.

No advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no session recording, no CDN that logs visitors.

4. Cookies and local storage

MEMAR uses a single HttpOnly cookie (memar_refresh) to maintain your login session. No tracking cookies. No third-party cookies.

5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because your data lives on your server, you control it completely:

  • Access: all data is in the SQLite file you own.
  • Deletion: delete the database file. Your account is gone.
  • Portability: use sqlite3 to export any table.
  • Correction: edit records directly or via the admin UI.

For billing-related data held by Stripe (invoices, payment methods), contact info@stratum.industries and we will initiate a Stripe data deletion request on your behalf.

6. Data retention

You control retention. MEMAR does not delete your data on any schedule. You may prune the database, run scripts/backup.sh, or configure your own retention policy.

7. Security

MEMAR uses bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication with rotating refresh tokens, HttpOnly session cookies, and security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options). Network-level security (TLS, firewall) is your responsibility as the operator.

8. Children

MEMAR is industrial fleet monitoring software intended for professional use. It is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes. Material changes will be announced via the MEMAR GitHub releases page.
